Terms of Service

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Membership

Membership in LibrarySphere is open to all eligible individuals. Members must maintain an active membership status to borrow books, access e-books, and place reservations. Membership is valid for one year from the date of registration.

2. Borrowing Rules

Standard members may borrow up to 3 physical books at a time for a period of 14 days. Premium Scholar members may borrow up to 5 books. Books may be renewed up to 2 times if no reservations are pending.

3. Overdue Fines

A fine of ₹5 per day is charged for each overdue book. Members with outstanding fines exceeding ₹100 may have their borrowing privileges suspended until fines are cleared.

4. Reservations

Members may reserve books that are currently checked out. Reserved books are held for 2 days once available. Unclaimed reservations are automatically expired and the book is offered to the next patron in the queue.

5. E-Book Access

E-book access is provided for online reading through the portal. Download permissions vary by title. E-book access requires an active membership. All e-book activity is logged for licensing compliance.

6. Acceptable Use

Members are responsible for all materials borrowed under their account. Lost or damaged books must be reported immediately. The library reserves the right to suspend accounts for violations of these terms.

7. Changes

LibrarySphere reserves the right to modify these terms at any time. Members will be notified of significant changes through the portal notification system.
