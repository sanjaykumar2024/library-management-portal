Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

LibrarySphere collects your name, email address, phone number, and address for membership management purposes. Borrowing and reading activity is logged to provide personalized services and generate usage reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively for library services including membership management, book circulation tracking, reservation processing, fine management, and e-book access control. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

3. Data Retention

Membership data is retained for the duration of your active membership plus 2 years. Loan and reservation records are kept for 5 years for audit purposes. E-book access logs are retained for 1 year.

4. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the library administration. Members can update their contact information directly through their profile page.

5. Security

All data is stored securely with role-based access controls. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. Staff access to member data is limited to authorized librarians and administrators.

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the Library Administration at the front desk or email privacy@librarysphere.edu.
