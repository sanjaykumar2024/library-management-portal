Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Click the Register button on the home page. Fill in your username, email, and password. Your membership will be activated immediately with a Standard tier and a unique membership card number.

Standard members can borrow up to 3 books simultaneously. Premium Scholar members can borrow up to 5 books. The loan period is 14 days per book with up to 2 renewals allowed.

If a book is checked out, click Join Waitlist on the book detail page. You'll be placed in a queue. When the book is returned, it's automatically held for the next person in line. You'll receive a notification and have 2 days to pick it up.

Browse Online E-Books or Free Classic Books from the navigation menu. Click on any title and press Read Online Now to open the reader.

A fine of ₹5 per day is calculated when you return an overdue book. You can view and pay fines through the Borrow & Return page or your dashboard.

Navigate to your My Profile page from the top-right menu. Click the Edit Profile button to update your details.

Go to your My Profile page. Your library card is displayed with your membership number, QR code, and tier. You can print it or scan it at the self-checkout kiosk.
Still need help? Visit the library front desk or email help@librarysphere.edu for assistance.
